How it works

One link. That's the whole setup.

Cue reads the calendar feed Canvas already gives every student. No password, no school approval.

  1. Copy your Calendar Feed link

    In Canvas, open Calendar and click Calendar Feed at the bottom right.

  2. Paste it into Cue

    Pick your reminder times. Turn on the daily digest if you want it.

  3. Click the menu bar icon

    Everything due shows up, and it stays in sync on its own.

FAQ

Questions

Is this made by Instructure?

No. Cue for Canvas is an independent app built by a student. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Instructure, the company behind Canvas.

Does it work with my school?

If your school uses Canvas, yes. Every Canvas account has a Calendar Feed link, and that's all Cue needs.

Do I need to give it my Canvas password?

No. Cue never asks for your password. It only reads your calendar feed.

Can it show whether I've submitted something?

Yes, if your school lets students create a Canvas API token. It's optional. Many schools don't allow it, and Cue works fine without it.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

When can I get it?
