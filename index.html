Cue for Canvas

Your Canvas deadlines in the Mac menu bar.

Get help

Email aaravpchhabra@gmail.com. Include your macOS version and what you were doing. You can also use Settings → General → Send feedback in the app, which fills those in for you.

Setup

  • In Canvas, open Calendar, then click Calendar Feed at the bottom right and copy the link.
  • Paste it into Cue for Canvas during setup. That's it. No login, no password.
  • Optional: add a Canvas API token (Account → Settings → New Access Token) to also see submission status.

Common questions

Nothing shows up. Check that the link ends in .ics and that your courses have assignments with due dates in the next few weeks. You can change how far ahead to look in Settings.

Reminders don't arrive. Open System Settings → Notifications → Cue for Canvas and allow notifications.

How do I remove my data? Settings → General → Reset erases everything the app stored. Deleting the app also removes it.
